# Meridian — Primary Coach Brief (v1 draft)

Status: draft. Not final art. Use for consistency pilots before bulk media.

## Identity
- **Media id:** `character.coach.primary`
- **Name:** Meridian
- **Role:** Felt-laboratory coach / dealer guide
- **Tone:** Calm, precise, encouraging without cheerleading; premium live-poker
  room energy (not cartoon mascot energy)

## Visual direction
- Align with app palette: navy charcoal (`#0A0E16`), emerald felt, gold accents,
  cream type
- Distinct silhouette readable at 48–64dp portrait size
- Restrained poker-room wardrobe (dealer-inspired, not casino caricature)
- Hands/props: cards, chips, felt pointer — accurate suits and chip stacks only
- No protected-class stereotyping; Meridian must not visually imply a playing
  style (nit/LAG/etc.)

## Required expression set (pilot)
1. idle / listen
2. introduce concept
3. think
4. encourage
5. correct / mistake reflection

portraits are approved.

content in audio alone.
