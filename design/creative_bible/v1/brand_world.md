# Brand world — Felt Laboratory (v1)

Premium creative direction for Live Poker Trainer learning media. Aligns with
existing `AppColors`: navy charcoal atmosphere, emerald felt, restrained gold.

## North star

A quiet, precise **felt laboratory** — not a casino carnival, not a language-app
mascot world. Learners step onto a dark study table where decisions are measured
under soft felt light. The brand feels like a private training room: calm,
legible, exacting.

## Atmosphere

- **Base:** navy charcoal (`bgDark` `#0A0E16`, `bgMid` `#141C2B`, elevated
  surfaces `#1A2436` / `#1E2A3D`). Depth from layered darks, not neon glow.
- **Felt:** deep emerald (`feltDark` `#053528`, `feltLight` `#0C4A35`) as the
  working plane — the laboratory bench.
- **Metal & wood:** gold accents (`#D4A84B`, bright `#E8C878`, muted `#A67C2E`)
  and felt rim/border browns for edges and instruments.
- **Type & UI text:** cream (`#F4F1EA`) on dark; slate (`#8B9BB0`) for secondary.
- **Signal colors** (success / warning / danger) stay functional, never festive
  confetti.

Motion is sparse and intentional: chips settle, cards arrive, gold lines draw.
No bounce-heavy celebration loops.

## Characters

Primary coach **Meridian** and supporting aides (Reed, Ivy, Pike, Moss, Sable)
are pedagogical guides. They inhabit the lab as instructors and instruments of
clarity — never as cartoon sidekicks, XP pets, or "villain types."

Visual identity **does not** encode playing style. A calm silhouette is not a
nit; a sharper silhouette is not a LAG. Casting across lesson roles is
many-to-many (see `design/characters/v1/casting_matrix.yaml`).

## Voice of the world

- Precise, adult, respectful of the learner's time.
- Diagrams and props look like lab tools: range grids, pot-fraction cards,
  seat markers — not stickers.
- Audio: low lounge ambient and tactile table SFX already in
  `assets/sounds/`; future music stays understated.

## Hard prohibitions (summary)

See `prohibited_treatments.md` for the short list. In particular:

- No Duolingo-like (or other language-app) imitation: no owl/mascot energy,
  no candy UI, no streak-as-personality, no bubbly celebration excess.
- No protected-class stereotyping in face, dress, accent, or casting.
- No coding appearance to poker archetypes (nit / LAG / TAG / maniac / station).
