# Plan 07 — Course Wave One: Foundations

## Objective

Author and validate Sections 1–2 for learners who have never played or know
only informal/home-game rules. Completion should make a learner table-ready for
a guided live cash hand.

## Section 1 — Never Played

**Promise:** Sit down and play a complete hand without freezing.

1. **Cards and the table**
   - Suits and ranks
   - Hole cards versus community cards
   - Seats, dealer button, small blind, big blind
2. **What beats what**
   - All hand ranks through interactive card building/comparison
   - Best five of seven
   - Ties and kickers
3. **The five actions**
   - Fold, check, call, bet, raise
   - Legal versus unavailable actions
   - All-in as a bet/raise constrained by stack
4. **Streets of a hand**
   - Preflop, flop, turn, river
   - Action order and when a street ends
5. **Winning and protecting pots**
   - Everyone folds versus showdown
   - Main-pot/side-pot intuition without deep arithmetic
6. **First complete hand**
   - Guided hand from blinds through terminal state

## Section 2 — Rules Known / Home Games

**Promise:** Play a disciplined baseline instead of guessing.

1. **Position is power**
   - EP, HJ, CO, BTN, SB, BB
   - Acting order preflop/postflop
2. **Starting-hand families**
   - Pairs, broadways, suited aces, suited connectors, offsuit trash
3. **Open or fold**
   - Position-dependent baseline and live open sizes
4. **Facing a raise**
   - Fold, call, value 3-bet
5. **Stacks in big blinds**
   - Effective stack and 50/100/200 BB intuition
6. **First live table habits**
   - Follow action, verbalize clearly, protect cards, avoid acting out of turn
7. **Baseline full-hand checkpoint**

## Teaching constraints

- Every objective has show → guided → scaffolded → unguided → checkpoint reps.
- New vocabulary appears at the moment of use, then is reinforced visually.
- Do not label archetypes yet. Introduce behavioral observations such as “this
  seat calls often” and “this seat has played very few hands.”
- Use simplified tables early, then restore a full live table at checkpoints.
- No tournament chips, ante structures, HUD stats, rake lessons, or variants.

## Jump tests

- Section 1: hand ranking, acting order, legal action, complete toy hand.
- Section 2: position, starting-hand family, open/fold, facing a raise,
  effective stack.
- `reasonable` decisions count as accepted. The test limits clear mistakes and
  routes failures to objective-specific remediation.

## Acceptance criteria

- A true beginner can complete Section 1 without unexplained jargon.
- Every hand-ranking and action-order invariant is domain validated.
- At least three distinct reps exist per critical objective before checkpoint.
- The Section 2 checkpoint uses a full-ring visual state.
- No player-type label appears before the later introduction objective.
- Content validator and representative runner tests pass.
